# Still Play Consumer Marketing Plan

## Positioning
- **Category:** Prediction market / consumer fintech.
- **Audience:** Retail bettors and prediction users who need fast, responsible access to short-term credit.
- **Core promise:** "Take your position when it matters, without breaking your cash flow."

## Messaging Framework
- **Primary user pain:** Missed opportunities, poor timing, and unmanaged risk.
- **Value proposition:** Fast access, transparent terms, and confidence in repayment flow.
- **Trust anchors:** Secure infrastructure, clear repayment terms, and partner integrations.
- **Main CTA language:** `Start early access`, `View demo`, `Join waitlist`.

## Funnel-Aligned Content (CCM)
- **Top of funnel (Awareness):**
  - Short-form explainer clips ("How prediction credit works in 30 seconds").
  - Social posts on market timing and disciplined participation.
  - SEO blog topics around prediction market onboarding and responsible play.
- **Middle of funnel (Consideration):**
  - "How it works" walkthroughs and comparison content.
  - User stories with outcomes (speed, clarity, and confidence).
  - Email education sequence for waitlist subscribers.
- **Bottom of funnel (Decision + Post-sale):**
  - Demo-led conversion pages.
  - FAQ-led objection handling.
  - Onboarding checklist and post-signup activation emails.

## Landing Page Content Goals
- Hero: Lead with user outcome and immediate CTA.
- User social proof: Show evidence users care about (speed, simplicity, confidence).
- How it works: 4-step flow + conversion CTA.
- Product-in-use: Demo preview to reduce uncertainty.
- Partner trust block: Separate from user social proof.
- FAQ + newsletter: Capture intent and nurture non-immediate converters.

## 30-Day Execution Plan
- **Week 1:** Finalize voice/tone, publish redesign, ship FAQ + demo journey.
- **Week 2:** Launch 3 TOFU pieces + 2 MOFU explainers + first email nurture.
- **Week 3:** Publish user-case content and optimize CTA copy from click data.
- **Week 4:** Run A/B tests on hero headline, CTA labels, and social-proof ordering.

## KPIs
- Waitlist conversion rate from landing sessions.
- Demo click-through rate.
- FAQ-to-signup conversion rate.
- Newsletter subscribe rate.
- Cost per qualified signup (when paid channels begin).
